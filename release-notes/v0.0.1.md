# elfhub 0.0.1 — first release

elfhub is a voice-first companion for your Mac. Hold **ctrl + option**, ask about what's on your screen, and it answers out loud, points at what matters, draws on the screen — and, when you let it, does the clicking and typing for you, step by step.

## What's in it

- **Your models, your choice.** Speech-to-text, the language model and the voice are each yours to pick: a cloud service with your own key, your ChatGPT plan ("Continue with ChatGPT"), or models running on this Mac — Ollama, Apple's on-device speech and voices, or speech and voice models you download (Whisper, Parakeet, Kokoro, Qwen3-TTS).
- **Elves.** Name your main elf, add helpers like Researcher and Writer, each with its own notes, skills and voice. Hand them longer jobs to do in the background, chat with each in its own thread, and share them as `.elf` files.
- **Help on screen.** Pointing, boxes, arrows and notes drawn right where you're looking, and a notepad for steps and commands.
- **Doing things for you.** With action mode on, elfhub works through tasks step by step, checking the screen after each one. It asks before anything that sends, deletes or pays; press **Esc** — or say "stop" — to take over at any time.
- **Web search for elves**, with DuckDuckGo by default or the service you choose.

## Privacy

There's no elfhub server, account or analytics. Your keys stay in your Keychain and your chats on your Mac. Password managers and private websites (banks, patient portals) are left out of screenshots, and card, insurance and health numbers are blacked out before a screenshot leaves your Mac. elfhub checks GitHub once a day for a newer version (you can turn that off in Settings › General).

## Requirements

macOS 14.2 or later on Apple silicon (M1 or newer).

This is an early release — tell us what breaks.
