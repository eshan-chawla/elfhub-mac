# elfhub 0.1.0 — public beta

The place for Elfs to live on your Mac. An Elf is an AI agent: it looks at your screen when you ask, talks with you, points at what matters, and acts only when you say so. This is the first public release. It's a beta, so tell us what breaks; the elfhub Discord is the quickest way (Support, at the bottom of the sidebar).

## What's new since 0.0.1

**Your Elfs**
- **Elfs, not elves.** The app calls them Elfs everywhere. Your main Elf sits at the top of the sidebar; click it to pick up your latest chat with it. Your other Elfs fold away below it.
- **The main Elf remembers you.** Once a chat has been quiet for half an hour, the main Elf folds what it learned about you, and what you talked about, into its memory (`user.md`). You can read, edit and undo every change on its page. Turn it off in Settings › Privacy & permissions.
- **A page for each Elf.** The main Elf's page shows your other Elfs and what each is doing, the work it handed out, its memory, files and changes. A side Elf's page has its chat and its work; settings open from the gear.
- **Pictures for Elfs.** Give any Elf a picture, or keep its initials.

**Talking and chatting**
- **Live captions** (optional, Settings › Controls) follow the voice word by word.
- **A natural voice.** Setup offers Kokoro, a voice that runs on your Mac (about 400 MB, downloaded from Hugging Face without an account). Without it, elfhub uses the most natural Apple voice on your Mac.
- **Pick ElevenLabs voices from a menu** instead of pasting IDs.
- **Chats name themselves** after the first reply (switch off in Settings › Privacy & permissions). Search all your chats, rename them, or delete them from the sidebar.
- **A fresh chat after a quiet half hour**, with a way back to the last one.
- **Stop a reply** with the button in the chat box, or Esc. A reply that fails now says why, with **Try again** and a shortcut to your model settings.

**Acting on your screen**
- **Approval in a popup** over whatever app you're in: Return to go ahead, Esc to cancel. "Dangerously allow everything" is there for when you're watching; it's never remembered after you quit.
- **Longer runs.** Choose 15 to 250 steps per task. Elfs can wait for a page to load, a download or a build to finish without using up steps.
- **Safer by default.** Quit, log out, delete and force-quit shortcuts always ask first, however the model writes them. Websites count as named only when you actually named them.

**Setup and settings**
- **Setup is six short steps,** one decision each, and picks up where you left it if you quit midway (macOS needs a restart for Screen Recording; there's a "Quit & reopen" button for that).
- **Settings in five pages:** General, Models, Tools & capabilities, Controls, Privacy & permissions.
- **Usage and cost** (Settings › General): tokens and an estimated cost per model and kind of work, kept only on your Mac.
- **Long chats cost less.** Only the newest screenshots of a long screen run, and a trimmed history, are sent with each turn.
- **Reporting a problem.** When something unexpected goes wrong, elfhub offers to open a GitHub issue for you to read and edit first, with keys and personal details taken out. Nothing is sent unless you submit it.
- **A new look** that follows your Mac's light or dark appearance. Text is in the Mac's own font; Fraunces and JetBrains Mono are used for headings, keys and code. The Elf draws in orange; your own drawing is blue.

**Fixes**
- OpenAI's reasoning models (o-series, GPT-5) work with your key.
- Text-only models no longer need Screen Recording.
- Push-to-talk says why when it can't start, and a quick tap no longer cuts off the next recording.
- A model that declines a request says so, instead of leaving an empty reply.
- Offline, a request fails at once instead of thinking for minutes.
- Deleting a model, a key or a downloaded voice asks first.
- Saved model setups and Elf settings are never wiped by a file elfhub can't read.

## Good to know

- Your Elfs' folder moves once from `Application Support/elfhub/Elves` to `Application Support/elfhub/Elfs`, with chats, notes, skills, tasks and undo history. Each Elf's `profile.md` becomes `user.md`.
- If you open 0.0.1 again afterwards, it starts a new, empty `Elves` folder. Your Elfs stay safe in `Elfs`.

## Privacy

There's no elfhub server, account or analytics. Your keys stay in your Keychain and your chats on your Mac. What you ask goes only to the model you choose. That same model also gets two small requests in the background, both on by default and both switchable in Settings › Privacy & permissions: a quiet chat's text to build your main Elf's memory, and a new chat's first question and answer to name it. Voice and speech models download from Hugging Face only when you ask, without an account. elfhub checks GitHub once a day for a newer version (Settings › General).

## Requirements

macOS 14.2 or later on Apple silicon (M1 or newer).
