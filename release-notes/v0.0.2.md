# elfhub 0.0.2: a new look, an easier start, a natural voice

The place for Elfs to live on your machine. An Elf is an AI agent: it looks at your screen when you ask, points at what matters, and acts only when you say so.

## What's new

- **A new look.** elfhub desktop now matches elfhub.ai. It follows your Mac's light or dark appearance, with a quiet sidebar that does too. Text is in the Mac's own font, with the elfhub typefaces (Fraunces and JetBrains Mono, bundled under the SIL Open Font License) for headings, keys and code. Every Elf gets an avatar: a picture you choose, or its initials — your main Elf on the elfhub blue, the others in one of six colors.
- **The Elf's pen is orange.** Pointers, circles, arrows, highlights and notes on your screen are now Annotation Orange, with a light edge so they stay readable on any background. Orange is used for nothing else. Your own drawing while you hold the shortcut is now blue, so the two are never confused.
- **An easier start.** Setup is six short steps, one decision each:
  - Your Elf starts with a suggested name instead of "elf".
  - "Continue with ChatGPT" comes first. An API key or a model on this Mac is one click away.
  - You choose its voice, then confirm the shortcut and permissions.
  - Last, you decide what stays on this Mac.
- **A natural voice.** Setup offers Kokoro, a voice model that runs on your Mac. It's about 400 MB, downloaded from Hugging Face without an account. It downloads in the background while you finish setting up, and your Elf switches to it when it's ready. If you skip it, elfhub now speaks with the most natural Apple voice installed on your Mac instead of the system default.
- **Elfs, not elves.** The app calls them Elfs everywhere.

## Good to know

- Your Elfs' folder moves once from `Application Support/elfhub/Elves` to `Application Support/elfhub/Elfs`. Chats, notes, skills, tasks and undo history move with it.
- If you open 0.0.1 again after 0.0.2, it starts a new, empty `Elves` folder. Your Elfs stay safe in `Elfs`, and 0.0.2 keeps using them.
- The Researcher and Writer starter Elfs are now unchecked during setup. You can add them any time from the "+" next to Elfs.

## Privacy

There's no elfhub server, account or analytics. The natural voice is downloaded only if you leave its box ticked, and it runs on your Mac. What you ask goes only to the model you choose. That same model also gets two small requests in the background, both on by default and switchable in Settings › Privacy & permissions: a quiet chat's text, to build your main Elf's memory, and a new chat's first question and answer, to name it.

## Requirements

macOS 14.2+ on Apple silicon (M1 or newer).
